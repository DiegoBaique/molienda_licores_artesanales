## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a rustic-premium, artisanal spirit rooted in the volcanic foothills of Isnos, Huila, Colombia. It bridges traditional campesino heritage, trapiche panela cultivation, and copper still distillation with high-end editorial sophistication. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Dark Editorial: deep charred-oak and night tones provide a dramatic canvas for warm sugarcane amber and aged gold accents. The experience targets connoisseurs of artisanal spirits, hospitality curators, and cultural travelers seeking authenticity, craftsmanship, and terroir. Visual rhythms evoke the slow drip of copper alembics, textured fibrous cane, and wax-sealed heritage bottles.

## Colors
The palette celebrates the raw materiality of artisanal spirits:
- **Primary (#F9BA2E):** Aged golden aguardiente and crystalline amber rum, reserved for primary focal actions, active states, and decorative badges.
- **Secondary (#A05A2C):** Toasted panela and charred barrel wood, grounding secondary CTAs, section highlights, and rich framing accents.
- **Tertiary (#3D5A45):** Deep Andean sugarcane foliage and highland mist, providing organic depth to terroir indicators and sustainable harvest callouts.
- **Neutral (#0B0B0B):** Deep obsidian night, paired with Charred Oak (#181818) for layered surfaces.

Text utilizes Parchment Cream (#F5EFE6) for high-contrast reading without the harsh glare of pure white, preserving the moody, warm tavern atmosphere.

## Typography
Typography creates a deliberate dialogue between old-world distillery prestige and contemporary clarity:
- **EB Garamond** acts as the heraldic voice for hero titles, bottle names, origin narratives, and tasting notes. It carries literary gravitas, reminiscent of vintage spirit labels and traditional alchemy.
- **Manrope** delivers high functional legibility for technical alcohol-by-volume specs, product descriptions, interactive controls, and UI metadata. 
- All uppercase labels carry expanded letter-spacing (0.08em to 0.12em) to mimic embossed distillery seals and barrel engravings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for expansive visual storytelling and structured product showcases:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutter, 4rem outer margin. Generous vertical section padding (6rem to 8rem) creates an unhurried, luxurious cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutter, 2rem outer margin. Product cards reflow to 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutter, 1.25rem margin. Tasting cards and process timelines stack vertically into singular focused narratives.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through subtle surface luminance shifts and warm ambient glows rather than aggressive drop shadows:
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background #0B0B0B with a subtle organic grain texture overlay.
- **Level 1 (Card & Containers):** Charred Oak (#181818) with a 1px border rendered in rgba(249, 186, 46, 0.12).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Modals):** #222222 with an amber-tinted bloom shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(229, 155, 32, 0.18)`.
- **Alembic Overlays:** Translucent surfaces use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` paired with `rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.75)` to simulate frosted amber and smoky glass.

## Shapes
A disciplined, architectural Soft roundedness (0.25rem / 4px) is utilized across the system. It references hand-cut label stock, woodblock prints, and rigid glass spirit packaging. Micro-elements like pills and sensory badges use 2px micro-radii to maintain structured elegance, intentionally avoiding overly playful circular designs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in #F9BA2E with #0B0B0B bold text in Manrope. Subtle inset border highlight. Hover shifts to panela amber (#E59B20) with a warm radiant glow.
- **Secondary / Craft Outline:** Transparent background with 1px border in #A05A2C, text in Parchment Cream (#F5EFE6). Hover fills with `rgba(160, 90, 44, 0.2)`.

### Cards (Spirits & Macerations)
- Built on Level 1 (#181818) with a delicate top border accented in alembic copper. Features an image stage for artisanal bottles, an origin badge (e.g., "Isnos - Caña Huilense"), technical metadata chips (ABV%, Aging, Botanical maceration), and an EB Garamond title.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact tags with 2px corner radius. Terroir chips use Tertiary muted tones (`rgba(61, 90, 69, 0.3)` with green borders); limited editions use metallic amber borders with small caps text.

### Form Inputs
- Dark recessed inputs (#111111) with a 1px outline in #2A2A2A. Active focus reveals a sharp #F9BA2E outline with no displacement. Labels rest above the field in uppercase tracking.

### Tasting Notes & Terroir Modules
- Horizontal split indicators featuring copper-colored gauge bars or botanical illustration badges displaying sweetness, acidity, barrel toast, and botanical intensity.
